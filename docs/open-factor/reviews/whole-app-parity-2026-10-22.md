# Whole-app functional and UX parity audit, 22 October 2026

**Verdict: not one-to-one in functionality or user experience.** Open Factor has a persistent local training foundation, not the full MacroFactor Workouts 1.4.1 (130) feature set. The largest gaps are smart programming, metric-aware logging, dated body data and charts, rich exercise management, and configurable analytics/settings. Empty source screens do not establish the missing calculations.

## Baseline and scope

The audited Android application is `openfactor/app`, not the inherited Nuvio projects at the repository root. This worktree's HEAD, local `main`, and `origin/main` all identify [the baseline commit](https://github.com/aarsh21/open-factor/commit/8ae58e2cba9524467530095c34886441c30f6381). No implementation or `PARITY.md` edits belong to this audit.

Two active workstreams are **not accepted here**:

- The parent thread owns exercise-detail autoplay. Baseline `ExerciseMedia.kt:40–42,115–194` still starts with a photo and explicit Play.
- A separate weight-logging worktree owns body-data implementation. Baseline `AppData` has no dated body records, progress photos, or trend model. Future changes need their own save/reload, chart, export, and UI checks.

Keep the chosen Nuvio navigation and grouped Settings presentation, central plus, full-area demonstrations, 5X/2.5X/1X controls, and no timeline. Autoplay on opening detail is now required by the user, regardless of unresolved source autoplay evidence. Accounts, subscriptions, mandatory connectivity, and service/integrity gates are excluded. Local body data, charts, exports, and optional Health Connect are not excluded.

**UX parity is an acceptance requirement for every feature.** Matching a destination name or final value is insufficient: match observed entry points, step order, defaults, field/control behavior, sheet/page presentation, feedback, Save/Cancel/Back, return context and reopened state. The explicit presentation exceptions above do not waive the workflows inside them. Where retained evidence does not establish an interaction, mark it U rather than claim a match or invent a source rule.
