## Changes

- 1X, 2.5X and 5X, Play/Pause and Replay sit in a compact floating capsule over the top of the video. It reserves space for the side camera previews. Full-width framing and the repeating angle playlist remain intact; error/Retry stays directly above the picture.
- Workout-completion exercise cards have consistent full widths, weighted photo/name headers and aligned metric columns. Warmups, working sets, unilateral sides, Myo components, RIR and incomplete paired components preserve their recorded values. Top summary values have defined columns; the compact Sets label counts completed logical sets including warmups, with an explicit Completed sets accessibility description.
- Three dashboard counters have subtle surface fills, precise keylines, small metric icons, stronger tabular numerals and a quiet blue center. They remain counters, without invented targets or completion fractions.
- Log Photos first offers Front, Side and Back. Choosing a pose opens its camera; the editor never launches one automatically. Library selection, private full-resolution capture, date/notes and interrupted-import recovery remain available.

## Verification

- 151 release unit tests and release lint passed without errors.
- The original-static-pack API35 suite reported 28 tests: 27 passed and one optional provider-fixture test skipped. The final label correction separately passed both targeted summary regressions, including its completed-set accessibility description.
- New native regressions require both summary cards to be exactly 284dp wide in a 320dp viewport at 1.35 font scale, and separately verify row reachability, Repeat, unilateral/Myo details, incomplete status and actual RIR. Photo entry asserts all three poses are displayed while the activity remains resumed, before any camera launch.

Medallions and the floating speed controls remained usable at 1.35 font scale; all three speeds were selected during playback, with automatic camera transitions and Pause working.
- Signed-candidate API35 showed Front/Side/Back before any camera; choosing Back opened the native camera and attached only Back, then Save retained it. Later corrections shorten the summary label and explicitly focus a non-text control on photo entry, preventing Android 8 from opening Gboard over the pose names. Video, counter and capture/storage logic remain unchanged.
- The final photo-focus regression passed on both Android 8 and Android 15, requiring Notes to remain unfocused while all three poses are displayed. The final signed APK confirmed pose-first entry without the keyboard at 1.35 font scale on Android 8, and cancellation returned safely to the editor. That emulator's system camera did not produce a confirmation after Shutter, so capture/save is not claimed there.
- The final signed APK passed the full Android 15 flow: choose Side, open the native camera, capture, confirm, attach only Side and save. Camera frames are synthetic emulator imagery.
- [Final app-source CI](https://github.com/aarsh21/open-factor/actions/runs/40821782188) passed: 151 unit tests, lint, and 28 device tests on each of API26 and API35 (26 passed, two documented skips per device). The downloaded GitHub APK matches the final local artifact hash above.
