# Open Factor 0.3.0

Original exercise guidance is part of the main workout flow again. The library opens Guide, Muscles and History. The logger has a photo and a visible Guide action; closing its sheet returns to the same exercise and set values. Programs, templates, warmups, advanced sets, history, gym profiles, body data and backups remain available.

The APK contains original exercise photos, instructions, male/female muscle diagrams, body assets and optimized equipment previews. Videos retain their exact exercise/camera identities and play on demand from the HTTPS Hono/Node and private RustFS service. Opening a guide does not download a video. Playback has camera selection, pause, replay and speed controls, stops when backgrounded, and uses a 128 MiB LRU disk cache. Full-frame fitting preserves movement cues. Controls remain visible in shorter guide viewports and camera choices scroll without overlapping them.

## Review and scope

The interaction pass used [Emil Kowalski's design-engineering guidance](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/main/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md) and the repository's Impeccable surface contract. Independent review found two issues that were fixed before the final device pass:

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portrait demonstration cropped into its viewport | Full-frame preview and video fitting | Keeps feet, grip and overhead travel visible. |
| Short guide viewport could hide controls or overlap camera hit targets | Height follows the viewport; camera rail reserves the control area | Keeps playback actions reachable on shorter screens. |

This evidence covers the named emulator configurations and exercised journeys. It is not a claim of universal hardware compatibility or proof that every interaction is flawless. Devices need an available HEVC decoder for the original video format. Static guidance remains usable when streaming fails; retained complete cached data supports offline playback but may be evicted. The API 26 helper failed with exit 137 during a redundant Wi-Fi-enable command after successful app checks; Wi-Fi had remained unchanged, and further device actions stopped.

The recovered collection has videos for 619 of 1,345 exercises, not complete demonstration coverage for every catalog record. Media ownership remains separate from the code license.
