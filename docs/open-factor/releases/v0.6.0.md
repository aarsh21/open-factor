# Open Factor 0.6.0

Make it yours, then get training. Personal setup, useful Settings and exercise guidance that starts when you open it.

## Changes

- Three short setup steps collect your name, optional gender, weight and height, preferred units, weekly workout goal, effort-rating preference and rest timer. Raw inputs and the current step survive a restart; Back, Skip and later review preserve your draft.
- Health Connect is available inside setup. One Connect action opens Android consent, then imports permitted provider readings automatically. Imported weights keep their original dates; typing your own weight takes precedence. Setup never fabricates a new weigh-in from an older reading.
- Profile is a full editor with validation, explicit Save and discard confirmation. Weekly goals appear in Habits; the selected gender supplies the initial anatomy figure. Existing completed installations keep their data and are not forced through setup again.
- Settings groups the working controls under You, Training, App, Data and About. Unavailable language/energy choices, the ineffective bodyweight-contribution switch and the old setup ceremony are removed. Templates, programs, backups and actual training controls remain available.
- Visible exercise guides autoplay muted, loop their camera angles and keep the frameless player with top speed controls. Pause is respected across background visits. Leaving the guide or scrolling the player away releases playback resources; an interrupted load has an explicit Retry.
- The exercise catalog loads once per process on a worker thread. Search indexes and their default ordering are reused across visits, with bounded caching and invalidation when the catalog changes. The app reports fully drawn only after the catalog is ready.

## Review

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit-only welcome | Three focused profile and training steps | Collect useful choices without a program-creation detour |
| Separate connection journey | Native Health Connect consent directly from setup | Import existing readings with fewer taps |
| Unused and ineffective settings | Working controls in five clear groups | Make each destination useful |
| Play required on every guide | Muted autoplay while visible, with deliberate pause | Demonstrate the exercise immediately |
| Catalog and index work repeated | Background process cache and bounded index reuse | Keep startup and repeated navigation responsive |
